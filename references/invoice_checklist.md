# 📋 The 8 Non-Negotiable Amazon Invoice Verification Criteria

Amazon Seller Performance and Brand Registry teams reject over 70% of submitted invoices due to technical discrepancies. Every invoice submitted alongside a Plan of Action must satisfy all 8 rules below:

---

| # | Mandatory Criterion | Strict Amazon Verification Standard | Common Disqualifier / Trap |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Issue Date** | Must be issued within the trailing **365 calendar days** from the notice date. | Invoice dated 366+ days ago. |
| **2** | **Supplier Details** | Must display legal business name, physical street address, valid telephone number, tax ID (EIN/VAT), and live active website. | Supplier uses only a P.O. Box, or website is inactive/unreachable. |
| **3** | **Buyer Entity Match** | Buyer name and address must match the **Legal Entity Name** and **Business Address** on your Seller Central account with 100% exact character alignment. | Buyer name matches owner personal name instead of registered LLC/Corp entity. |
| **4** | **Quantity Verification** | Total units shown on invoices across trailing 365 days must **equal or exceed** your total Amazon sales volume for that ASIN in the trailing 90 days. | Invoice shows 10 units, but you sold 45 units on Amazon. |
| **5** | **Line Item Detail** | Must clearly state the item name, model number, SKU, or UPC/EAN corresponding directly to the flagged Amazon ASIN. | Vague generic descriptions (*"Assorted electronics"*). |
| **6** | **Document Format** | Must be an authentic issued **tax invoice / sales receipt** in non-editable PDF or high-resolution physical document scan. | Excel spreadsheets, Word `.docx`, screenshots of software dashboards. |
| **7** | **Forbidden Document Types** | Must NOT be a **pro-forma invoice**, commercial quotation, order confirmation, packing slip, or retail store receipt (Walmart, Target, Best Buy). | Pro-forma invoices are treated as unconfirmed intent to buy, not finalized purchase. |
| **8** | **Digital Authenticity** | Amazon cross-verifies supplier registry via phone calls and public tax registries. | Forged, digitally edited, or altered PDF metadata (results in permanent Section 3 ban). |

---

### Pro-Tip: Pre-Submission Document Packaging
When uploading invoices to Seller Central:
1. Highlight (using yellow digital highlighter) the matching ASIN, quantity, supplier contact, and buyer entity name.
2. If the supplier is an authorized distributor rather than the direct brand manufacturer, attach a **Letter of Authorization (LOA)** confirming their distributor rights.
3. Include supplier order tracking and bank transaction proof (cleared ACH/wire confirmation) if dealing with high-risk authenticity complaints.
