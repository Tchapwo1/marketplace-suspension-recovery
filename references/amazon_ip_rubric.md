# ⚖️ Amazon Intellectual Property (IP) Infringement Appeal Rubric

## 1. The 4 Categories of IP Infringement on Amazon

| Category | Typical Notice Language | Reinstatement Path |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Trademark Infringement** | *"Received a complaint from rights owner claiming unauthorized trademark use in title, bullet points, or brand field."* | 1. Retraction from rights owner, OR<br>2. Proof of First Sale Doctrine + authorized sourcing, OR<br>3. Complete catalog deletion + POA. |
| **Counterfeit Allegations** | *"Item offered on ASIN B0XXXXXXXX is suspected of being counterfeit."* | Strict 8-point commercial tax invoices + Letter of Authorization (LOA). |
| **Copyright / Image Strike** | *"Rights owner asserts copyrighted photographs or product copy were utilized without license."* | 1. DMCA Counter-Notice (if non-infringing), OR<br>2. Proof of licensing, OR<br>3. Removal of images + POA. |
| **Patent (Utility / Design)** | *"Complaint regarding US Patent #[XXXXXXX]."* | Amazon APEX (Neutral Patent Evaluation) or formal legal settlement/licensing. |

---

## 2. The Rights Owner Retraction Protocol
For trademark and copyright complaints, Amazon requires the original complainant to send a retraction directly to `notice-retraction@amazon.com`:
- Must include Complaint ID / Notice Reference.
- Must include Seller Merchant Token.
- Must originate from the exact email address that filed the original complaint.

---

## 3. The Reinstatement Strategy When Retraction is Impossible
If a brand refuses to retract or does not reply:
1. **Admit the listing workflow failure**: Acknowledge that the internal listing creation team failed to cross-reference the USPTO / WIPO trademark database prior to tagging the keyword.
2. **Delete the inventory**: Show proof of 100% catalog deletion (`sku_deleted_date`).
3. **Institute the Preventative Sourcing & Listing SOP**: Introduce a mandatory 3-step clearance protocol before any new brand or keyword is added to the catalog.
