# 🎨 Pillar 4: Output Design & Interaction Specification — Marketplace Suspension Recovery

## 1. Visual Hierarchy & Legal-Grade Markdown Styling
Because this skill produces high-stakes legal and operational correspondence, the output must radiate objective authority, rigorous precision, and compliance expertise:

- **Executive Tone**: Zero emotional pleading (*"We are very sorry, we love Amazon, please reinstate us"*). The language is that of an institutional COO or legal compliance officer (*"Root Cause Analysis: On March 14, 2026, our inventory intake department experienced a breakdown in barcode verification protocols..."*).
- **Typography & Formatting**: Clean GitHub Flavored Markdown with bold lead-ins, numbered chronological action steps, and structured tables.
- **Section Dividers**: Clean markdown horizontal rules (`---`) separating diagnostic analysis from the copy-pasteable submission text.

---

**Merchant Token / ID:** [Merchant ID]  
**Case / Notice Reference:** [Notice Reference ID]

### PART 1: THE ROOT CAUSE OF THE ISSUE
[Factual, non-defensive explanation of the exact internal workflow failure that triggered the deactivation.]

### PART 2: IMMEDIATE CORRECTIVE ACTIONS TAKEN
[Specific chronological remedial actions already completed prior to this appeal, citing ASINs, dates, and order IDs.]

### PART 3: PREVENTATIVE MEASURES & SYSTEMIC CONTROLS

### APPENDIX: SUPPORTING EVIDENCE & DOCUMENTATION

- Document 2: Internal SOP Manual v2.4 (Updated Barcode Scanning Procedures)
